# Post-Mortem Protocol

## Information-Set Lock

Freeze the analysis at the decision time.

Create two buckets:

### Known then
Information reasonably available before the decision.

### Known now
Subsequent information that must not contaminate evaluation of the original process.

## Evaluate three things separately

1. Decision quality
2. Outcome quality
3. Learning value

A good process can produce a bad outcome. A bad process can produce a good outcome.

## Questions

- What was the thesis?
- What evidence supported it then?
- What assumptions mattered?
- What alternatives existed?
- What would have invalidated the thesis then?
- Was sizing consistent with uncertainty?
- Which signal was misread?
- Which lesson generalizes, and which is hindsight-specific?
