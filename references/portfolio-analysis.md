# Portfolio Analysis

## Economic exposure over ticker count

Five tickers can represent one dominant economic bet. Analyze the underlying exposures, not just the number of securities.

Check:
- asset-class exposure
- sector exposure
- factor exposure
- geography
- currency
- liquidity
- macro sensitivity
- common catalysts
- leverage

## Concentration

Concentration is decision-changing when a single thesis, sector, factor, asset class, or macro regime can materially determine portfolio outcomes.

## Position sizing

Size should be analyzed relative to:
- total capital
- downside scenarios
- liquidity
- thesis confidence
- reversibility
- correlated exposures

Do not infer a user’s risk tolerance from identity or stereotypes. Use only what the user explicitly provides.

## Portfolio stress testing

For material portfolios, test relevant shocks such as:
- risk-off / liquidity contraction
- rates higher for longer
- crypto drawdown
- sector-specific shock
- company-specific earnings deterioration

If exact loss estimates require unavailable correlations or sensitivities, do not fabricate them. Describe exposure directionally and identify the missing inputs.

## Risk vs uncertainty

Risk concerns adverse outcomes that can be reasonably described or stress-tested. Uncertainty concerns incomplete knowledge about inputs, mechanisms, probabilities, or future states. Keep them separate in the diagnosis.
