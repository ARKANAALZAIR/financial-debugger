# Personal Finance Decision Layer

Use only the minimum relevant context.

Potential variables:
- emergency liquidity
- debt obligations
- time horizon
- near-term cash needs
- capital at risk
- existing exposure
- stated constraints
- tax considerations when explicitly relevant and verifiable

Separate:
- expected return
- ability to absorb loss
- need for liquidity

Do not equate “higher expected return” with “better personal decision.”
