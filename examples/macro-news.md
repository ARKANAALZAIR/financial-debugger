# Example — Macro News Debug

User: “Inflasi turun, berarti bank sentral akan cut rate, yield turun, lalu saham Indonesia naik.”

Expected diagnosis:
- Decompose the macro chain into links.
- Test each link rather than accepting the narrative as one fact.
- Check timing and alternative policy reactions.
- Distinguish lower inflation from guaranteed easing.
- Distinguish lower yields from guaranteed equity gains.
- Identify which Indonesian companies/sectors actually transmit the macro effect.
- If evidence is insufficient, retain uncertainty rather than forcing a market call.
