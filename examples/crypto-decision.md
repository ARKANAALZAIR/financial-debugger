# Example — Crypto Decision Debug

User: “BTC turun karena Fed hawkish, ETF masih inflow, jadi temporary dip. Gue mau tambah Rp50 juta.”

Expected diagnosis:
- The claim “Fed caused the decline” requires causal evidence.
- ETF inflows do not by themselves prove temporary price weakness.
- Long-term BTC thesis and short-term timing decision must be separated.
- Alternative explanations: liquidity, leverage unwind, risk-off positioning, regulatory news, market-wide moves.
- Portfolio impact should be evaluated if BTC/ETH already dominate exposure.
- Counterfactual: if easing does not arrive, does the decision still make sense?
